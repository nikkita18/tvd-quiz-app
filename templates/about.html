About The Vampire Diaries

The Legacy

The Vampire Diaries tells a story drenched in blood, love, and betrayal. Set in Mystic Falls, it follows Elena Gilbert as she finds herself tangled between two immortal brothers: Stefan, the noble, and Damon, the dangerous. As secrets unfold, friendships are tested, and hearts broken — the town’s supernatural history begins to awaken.

🕯️ The Mystic Falls Grimoire 🕯️

Magical conduits, ancient seals, and lethal relics that shaped the eternal struggle between predators and prey.

Daylight Rings

Species: Vampire

Enchanted lapis lazuli rings crafted by Bennett Witches, allowing vampires to walk under sunlight without burning.

Active Protection: Immunity to solar combustion.

Gilbert Ring

Species: Human Only

Protects the human wearer from death caused by any supernatural entity. However, repeated deaths chip away at sanity.

Resurrection: Instant revival from supernatural trauma.

White Oak Stake

Species: Original Vampire Killer

Carved from the singular ancient White Oak tree used in the spell of creation. The only weapon that can burn an Original to ash.

Lethal Threat: Absolute ash combustion on contact.

The Ascendant

Species: Gemini Coven Key

A celestial key linked to the power of a Gemini Witch and a solar eclipse, capable of opening the gateway to Prison Worlds.

Dimensional Gate: Exiles targets to eternal prison cycles.

⚜️ Chronology of Blood ⚜️

Follow the dark milestones that stained the history of Mystic Falls over a thousand years.

1000 AD
Birth of the Originals

To protect her family from werewolves, Esther Mikaelson uses a dark solar spell to forge the first vampires.

1864 AD
The Battle of Mystic Falls

Founding Families trap and burn 27 vampires. Stefan and Damon Salvatore are turned by Katherine Pierce.

2009 AD
The Return & Awakening

Stefan Salvatore returns to Mystic Falls to find Elena Gilbert, triggering the return of Damon and the awakening of tomb vampires.

2018 AD
The Salvatore School

Alaric and Caroline co-found the Salvatore Boarding School to protect and mentor the next generation of supernatural youths.
